Topic_description:
Create a simple educational poster explaining why AI sometimes gives incorrect or made-up information. Explain that AI generates responses by predicting likely patterns based on its training and the information available in the conversation or connected sources. It does not automatically verify whether every statement is true. Show common causes such as missing information, ambiguous questions, outdated information, insufficient context, and incorrect assumptions. Keep the language very simple and suitable for a company-wide AI Tech Byte. Avoid complex technical terminology.

However, the restaurant may actually be closed, the information may be outdated, or the restaurant may not have the claimed menu options.
